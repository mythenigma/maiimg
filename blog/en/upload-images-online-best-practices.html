How the share should work

Prepare

Choose files before you upload

A good share starts before the upload button. Remove duplicates, put the clearest image first, and split unrelated groups into separate links.

Share

Use the generated link and QR code

After upload, use the direct link for chat or email and the QR code for in-person or printed access. Both point to the same share.

Control

Avoid forever links by default

For personal or temporary shares, set an expiry or view limit. If the audience or context changes, revoke the link and create a cleaner share.

Checklist

  • Remove duplicates before uploading.
  • Keep one share to one purpose or audience.
  • Stay within 25 files per share.
  • Put the most important image first.
  • Use QR only when scan access helps.
  • Set expiry or revoke for temporary shares.
