How the share should work

Listing package

One link for the photos that matter

A property share works best when it is not a giant archive. Put the useful photos in order, keep the set under 25 files, and make the first image obvious.

QR handoff

Make print material scan-ready

The same MaiIMG share gives you a direct link and QR code. Put the QR on open-house material or a window card, while using the link in messages and email.

Access lifecycle

Close stale listing photos

When photos are replaced or a listing is no longer active, close the previous link. This reduces confusion and keeps old versions from being forwarded later.

Checklist

  • Create one share per property, update, or review context.
  • Keep each share under 25 files so it opens quickly.
  • Use a direct link for messages and email.
  • Use the generated QR code for open-house or print material.
  • Set expiry around the listing cycle or review window.
  • Revoke older links after a listing update or sale.
